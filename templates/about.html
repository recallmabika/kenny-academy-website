About the college

An icon for Chipinge and Manicaland, built on results.

Kenny Technologies Group of Colleges runs two campuses — Kenny Academy Rimbi and Kenny Academy Rimai — teaching Form 1 to 6 alongside Computer Science and HEXCO courses. The same discipline shows up on the exam register and on the sports field.

Two campuses, one standard

Kenny Academy Rimbi and Kenny Academy Rimai operate under Kenny Technologies Group of Colleges, sharing a curriculum, a coaching structure and a director. Students move between full-time academics and competitive sport without either one being treated as the lesser priority.

That balance produced back-to-back results at the National Games: trophies in 2024, a Certificate of Excellence in 2025, and individual honours for three students recognised as best in their category.

  • Kenny Academy Rimbi
    Form 1–6, full-time & extra lessons    Chipinge
  • Kenny Academy Rimai
    Form 1–6, Computer Science & HEXCO    Chipinge

Financial honesty, undiminished ambition

Not every season has gone to plan. Financial constraints kept the college from travelling to a tournament in Uganda — a setback the director has spoken about openly rather than around. It hasn't slowed the ambition: Kenny Technologies Group of Colleges is defending its Under 15 netball title next term and continuing to compete at the ASSFA Festival Tournament, where it first represented the region in 2023.

The college welcomes sponsorships and partnerships from anyone who wants to back that ambition — sports kit, travel costs, or academic equipment all move the same goal forward.

"Kenny Technologies Group of Colleges has emerged as an icon for Chipinge and Manicaland — in sport and in academics. Every organiser, coach and player behind these results has our appreciation, and our next target is already set."
— Prof Caleb Simango, on the college's 2024–2025 season
